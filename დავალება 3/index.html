About Us

Ferrari is one of the world's most iconic luxury sports car manufacturers.
The Italian company was founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939 and is headquartered in Maranello, Italy. Ferrari is widely recognized for its powerful engines, innovative technology, elegant design, and exceptional performance.
The company has a long and successful history in motorsport, especially in Formula 1, where Ferrari has competed since the championship's beginning in 1950. Today, Ferrari produces high-performance sports cars and grand tourers that combine advanced engineering with Italian craftsmanship. The Ferrari brand represents speed, innovation, luxury, and a strong passion for motorsport.
